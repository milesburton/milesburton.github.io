= Upgrading the Aspire One D257 RAM to 2GB =
## You'll need
- Small phillips screw driver
- Old credit card
- 2 GB DDR3 SO-DIMM (4Gig doesn't work, I found out the expensive way)

## Step One:
Remove the keyboard. There are 3 tab locks at the top of the keyboard (by the function keys). Push these in about 1mm, that'll release the keyboard. It may take a little bit of jimmying for the keyboard to popout.

When the keyboard is loose you'll see the ribbon connecting to the board. The socket has a little plastic lock which needs to be opened upwards. No force is required be careful.

## Step two:
Once the keyboard is removed you'll see a number of screws marked "door" (see below). Unscrew each of these.

![D257 TOP](/wiki-media/D257_TOP.JPG)
![D257 Hard Disk](/wiki-media/D257_Hard_Disk.JPG)
![D257 TOP LEFT](/wiki-media/D257_TOP_LEFT.JPG)

## Step three:
Spin the laptop over. The door, once the screws have been removed is clipped into place (arrrg!). It pivots from the front of the laptop backwards. 

Grab your credit card and carefully get it under the right hand side at the back. Push the door away from you as the clips lock outwards (aka, you need to bend it a bit). Work your way around from back to front until the panel comes off. Be really careful as you can break the clips. That said the screws will hold the door securely regardless.

![D257 BOTTOM2](/wiki-media/D257_BOTTOM2.JPG)
![D257 BOTTOM](/wiki-media/D257_BOTTOM.JPG)

## Step five:
Replace the ram by unclicking the two locks on either side of the module. Carefully replace yours. If you're feeling brave it may be worth firing up the laptop to make sure it accepts the module before putting it back together and finding it doesn't work.

## Step six:
Clip the door from front to back. You may need to carefully bend the door to get it to fit correctly. Make sure you have an even lock afterwards by visually checking all the clip points.

## Step seven:
Reinstall the screws you removed. Make sure you haven't knocked the touch-pad ribbon by clicking it down securely.

## Step eight:
Pivot the keyboard in from the front. Carefully replace the ribbon in the socket and secure the latch. Once you are happy carefully lower the keyboard back into position clicking the three tabs you unlocked earlier. The keyboard will once again sit flush with the case. 

## Fire it up!
No more modifications are required. Power it up and enjoy your new ram.
