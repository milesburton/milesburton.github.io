# Fixing Hyundai i20 Rear washer


Very quick one. My 2014/15 Hyundai i20 rear washer just randomly stopped working some time ago. I just manged to get it working.

If you have the same problem. First of all use a small needle and check if there's a blockage in the jet. If this doesn't work, open the boot and locate the hose leading to the jet. Between the main hose and the jet hose is a little connector. Pull these apart (it's fine don't worry). Remove the spring that comes out. Here's the disgusting bit. Try blowing the jet hose, and see if it expels air normally - if so it's probably fine. On the main hose, gentle suck, this will unclog the blockage (you may get a mouthful of washer fluid, so prepare yourself). Connect them back together and try your rear washer again, repeat the process if it doesn't immediately work. 

Chances are that's your issue
