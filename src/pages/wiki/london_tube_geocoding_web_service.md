# London Tube Geocoding web-service

<p class="wiki-date">Earliest known revision <time datetime="2010-04-02T22:59:38Z">2 Apr 2010</time></p>


## Introduction
The London tube Geocoding service is a simple ASMX (SOAP) web-service which allows you to pass a station name string and retrieve the associated spherical coordinates.

You may access the service [here](http://services.mnetcs.com/TubeGeocoder/)
