## Background
Nightingales are an established motorcycle dealer based in Rugby. Even though Nightingales are a fairly small dealer they have quite an impressive range of bikes & accessories. 

As with most diverse companies they have recently moved to the web selling a variety of different motorcycle accessories. They can be found both through [Ngales.com](http://www.ngales.com/) or their [Ebay store](http://stores.ebay.co.uk/Nightingales-Motorcycles) in the UK.

![Nightingales Rugby Small](/wiki-media/Nightingales_Rugby_Small.jpg)

## My Experiences
What brought me to Nightingales was mostly nostalgia. Many years ago I bought my very first motorbike with them. I must admit I was very indifferent at the time - they had the bike I wanted for the right price. The bike itself, a CG125, lasted perfectly for two years until I downgraded to four wheels.

Back to the present; Ngales.com have some excellent prices [at the time of writing] on a range of gear. I was interested in a pair of Phantom Textile trousers which were around £30 less than their competitors and significantly less than the brick and mortar counterparts.

![Buffalo-Phantom-Motorcycle-Textile-Trousers](/wiki-media/Buffalo-Phantom-Motorcycle-Textile-Trousers.jpg)

This is where my story begins. Having recently purchased a new bike I was urgently kitting up against the clock. I needed to pick-up the bike the following week; no problem I thought, how long does it take to ship a pair of 'in-stock' trousers via courier? 

Unfortunately, their stocking system isn't real-time. A week had past without any indication they'd be late. I tried emailing them several times - no response (which seems to be very common with online stores!). With the impending bike pick-up on my mind I decided to call them. In all honesty, they were polite. Supposedly the recent Icelandic Volcano (cough) had delayed shipments and they'd get the trousers out the next day.

Thankfully, they arrived on the day I picked up my bike! Sadly something was a little 'off' - the large trousers I bought looked better suited to a clown (XXL?)! Fair enough I thought, serves me right for ordering online. Once again I naively tried to drop them a quick mail (yes, I know. I need to wean myself off email) - no surprise, I didn't receive a response. After returning with the bike in my jeans (sigh) I gave them call, they told me to simply send them back.

Eventually I did receive the replacement in the correct size. Strangely though I had to foot the return fee (£6.80!) - no responses from their team.

My advise would be, stay clear of Nightingales unless you can see them in person. It's a real shame, I like to support smaller stores but this really turns me away from them. Hopefully I was just a 'One-off' and they'll improve - I doubt it though, they've been around long-enough.
