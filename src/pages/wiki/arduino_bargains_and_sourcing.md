## Various Sources
[China] [SeeedStudio](http://www.seeedstudio.com/depot) - They have some great deals and discounts on various electronics. Not everything is cheap though and if anything goes wrong it's quite expensive to ship back

[UK] [YeRobot](http://www.yerobot.com/index.php) - 'Offical' distributors of DF-Robot (Chinese designer of electronics). Some good bits and peices. Good support!

[UK] [NUElectronics](http://www.nuelectronics.com/estore/index.php?main_page=index) - Cheap source of some good parts like LCD Shields and Ethernet. Very stale page though, not much in the way of new products (in over a year).

[UK] [Earthshine-Design](http://earthshinedesign.co.uk/) - Hobby Electronics Supplier, some reasonble prices on the Freeduino clones and a bunch of intereesting products and supplies.

[UK] [Tinker-tools](http://tinkerit.myshopify.com/collections/for-beginners/products/beginners-kit) - Offical Arduino distributor (and designers!), stocks all the main stuff :)

[UK] [Batsocks](http://www.batsocks.co.uk/) - Hobby "homemade" electronics, great for those one off custom products! TVShield for example.

[??] [Deal Extreme](http://www.dealextreme.com/products.dx/category.399) - Free delivery, good for those misc products. Again, not everything is cheap - the exception rather than the rule (ie Touchscreens!)

[UK] [Farnell](http://uk.farnell.com) - Commerical [wholesale?] Electronics supplier. Very good prices, free delivery over 5-6 quid. Handy for optocouplers or any other ICs you need!
