## Abstract
Over the last two decades we have seen the introduction and advancement of technologies which our ancestors would have never dreamed possible. Technologies such as The Internet, GPS and mobile computing have enabled people to explore and interact with our world. Information that once was static has the potential to be as dynamic and a free as the person using it. Yet ironically, the proliferation of localised positioning systems which can be used in buildings has yet to be seen.

The IEEE 802.11 standard, known as Wi-Fi, provides the perfect basis onto which a positioning platform can be built. Wi-Fi is cheap to implement and ubiquitous in most office and academic environments. 
In this paper we investigate the potential for Wi-Fi to be used as a positioning system and how it is realised as GeoSpark, a prototype platform which provides positioning and dynamic mapping. 

### Keywords
Wi-Fi, Positioning Systems, Dynamic Mapping, Location Based Services, Geo-Informatics, Path-finding.
