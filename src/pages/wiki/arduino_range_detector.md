*Migrated from the old MediaWiki install. Images referenced in the original article were not recovered during migration and are marked below.*

This was a little experiment to combine my recent LCD shield and a previous project, the URM37 ultrasonic distance measurement IC.

Very simply I wanted a little bar to display how far away I was from the device and this is what I ended up with.
