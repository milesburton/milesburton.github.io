## Introduction
The Rainbowduino is a brand new, Arduino compatible, LED driver. It has two rows of PWM outputs which can be accessed through a shift-register. 

## Rainbowduino as a Meggy Jr
Based on the [Megga Jr code](http://code.google.com/p/meggy-jr-rgb/), enhanced by a [modification](http://www.seeedstudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=396) say hello to [Plasma](http://download.milesburton.com/Arduino/RainbowDuino/VIDEO_006.mp4) (Video 2MiB)
