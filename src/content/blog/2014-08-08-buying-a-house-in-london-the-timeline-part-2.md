<p>After my first very high level post I thought it would be worth digging into the real aspects of a property you should be looking at.</p>
<ol>
<li>Roof, is there a felt underlay? Can you see sunlight?</li>
<li>Garage, any examples of leakage? Subsidence?</li>
<li>Electrics &#8211; Properly grounded? How old is the box?</li>
<li>Are smoke alarms in all rooms or only the halls (particularly important if you want an extention)</li>
<li>*Any* Asbestos. If you want to do improvements this is a VERY expensive problem</li>
<li>Does the house have adiquet drainage? Are the Gutters draining to an appropriate location</li>
<li>Does the house have a combi boiler? (this could cost thousands to upgrade</li>
<li>Are there any redundant chimney stacks? They are expensive to remove and require maintenance if they are not in use</li>
<li>Are the light and socket boxes all properly aligned</li>
</ol>
<p>Bare these items in mind when you view a property</p>
