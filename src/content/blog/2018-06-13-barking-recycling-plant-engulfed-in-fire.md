<p>Photography is indeed a lottery, as I discovered earlier today. Unfortunately a fairly local Recycling plant suffered quite a nasty fire requiring engines from several nearby counties to respond.</p>
<p>Following a morning of shots, on my way home I discovered a huge plume of smoke, armed with my camera bag I took the above images. At the time of writing nobody has been hurt and the fire is now under control.</p>
