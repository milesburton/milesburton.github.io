<p>Like hundreds of thousands of &#8220;twenty-somethings&#8221; all over the UK I&#8217;ve always wanted to own a home. Yes yes, those who know me are used to seeing me move all over the place this my guilty goal. Owning a home, even if you rent it still means you&#8217;ve got your own little homested 😉</p>
<p>Before I began this process the key question I asked myself was what can I afford?  This question can be answered by calculating your savings (capital) vs income. The fastest way to do this is to contact a mortgage company and get a mortgage in principle. Just bare in mind a mortgage in principle is nothing more than a guideline and doesn&#8217;t mean you&#8217;ll get a mortgage of that value. When you work out what you can really afford, take out all emotion. This process has been at least 1/3 more expensive than I assumed &#8211; <strong>seriously, this is an expensive undertaking</strong></p>
<p><strong>tl;dr &#8211; you&#8217;re buying a £350,00 house. </strong></p>
<p><strong>The Brutal Version &#8211; What you will pay (my scenario):</strong></p>
<p>Lets assume you&#8217;re buying a fairly modest property in London for £350,000 (eep!)</p>
<p><strong>Immediate</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Mortgage in Principle &#8211; <strong>To reiterate, this is <em>totally worthless</em>. It means nothing. You may not get what is offered!</strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Initially (~circa one week of offering)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Mortgage arrangement fee: £1000~</li>
<li>Valuation/survey costs: £500</li>
<li>Conveyance/solicitors cost: £300</li>
</ul>
<p>Total = <strong>£1800</strong></p>
<p><strong>Medium term</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Additional survey costs: £800 &#8211; unless you&#8217;re a new build. GET A BUILDING SURVEY. They check the house wont fall down. Do it&#8230; seriously</li>
<li>Electrical, Gas/Central heating, Plumbing &#8211; £~300 &#8211; This is your choice but I&#8217;d suggest getting the GCG, Electrics and plumbing checked out. All three can be hugely expensive if its broken</li>
<li>Deposit to seller: Usually 10-20% of the agreed property price</li>
</ul>
<p>Total Cost: <strong>£36,100 (£1100 + £35,000)</strong></p>
<p><strong>Long Term</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Remaining solicitors costs (incl landregistry, drainage etc):<strong> £1300</strong> &#8211; You did check their costs right? It&#8217;s bloody hard work photocopying paper. <strong>CHECK YOUR SOLICITORS EXTRA COSTS</strong></li>
<li>Deposit to mortgage company: Dependant on mortgage company but usually the remaining 10% (for 20% deposit in total)  &#8211; <strong>£35000</strong></li>
<li>Stamp duty: Relative to price. Over 250k it&#8217;s 0.3%&#8230; In my case that&#8217;s around 10k  &#8211; <strong>£10500</strong></li>
<li>Initial mortgage payment. Usually 1.3x your quoted rate. It may be charged twice over be warned. <strong>Assume you have to pay 2.5x morgage payments up front. (£3,000)</strong></li>
<li>Buildings insurance: Relative to cost, usually<strong> £~3-500</strong></li>
<li><strong>Costs to repair building: CAN BE EXPENSIVE (my case is around £5k)</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Total: <strong>£58,300</strong></p>
<p><strong>Moving in costs</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>White van man: ~£200</li>
<li>White goods: Fridge £650. washer: £500, dryer: £460</li>
<li>Furniture: Bed + Mattress (they are separate!): ~ £700+. Sofa: ~£1500</li>
<li>Appropriate repayment of friends fuel and inebriation costs: £150 (don&#8217;t be a dick&#8230; get them drunk or pay them for their time)</li>
</ul>
<p>Total costs: <strong>£4,160</strong></p>
<p><strong>Misc costs</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Misc repairs on your rental property: £~100</li>
<li>Bullshit cleaning costs on your rental property: £200</li>
<li>Please bare in mind your time. They usually want you to be at your flat at a certain time. This is a resource (your time, or your money)</li>
</ul>
<p>Total costs: <strong>£300</strong></p>
<p><strong>GRAND TOTAL ESTIMATED CASH COSTS:  £100,660 // That means you need this in cash.. Now</strong></p>
