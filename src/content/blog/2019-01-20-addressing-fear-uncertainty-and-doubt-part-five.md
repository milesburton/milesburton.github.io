<p><em>(This article was originally written for a local publication)</em></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">If everyone drove EVs, we’d need to build 9 more power-stations! </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">My uncle, a retired Royal Dragoon who are well known for engineering, surprised me with this comment. He along with many others have been subtly fed half facts, strawman arguments and outright lies stemming way back to the very origins of the electric car when oil was becoming widespread.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">The reason behind the colossal quantity of anti-ev, and renewable propaganda is fairly easy to answer &#8211; money and power. Who controls the power, holds the power. By owning a ICE vehicle you are forever at the whims of Big Oil. When you introduce renewable energy, and alternative transport, we democratise the power &#8211; you no longer have to go cap in hand to some very scary people. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">Tesla are one of the first companies who truly threaten this power model. They have broken away from the ugly, ‘futuristic’, designs to produce a product that is actually desirable to the common man. The Model S, 3, X, Solar Roof &amp;  Powerwall are all beautifully designed, ready to be put bluetacked to your teenagers wall; the incumbents are absolutely terrified, so much so Tesla is the largest shorted </span><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2018/04/11/tesla-is-the-biggest-short-in-the-us-stock-market.html"><span style="font-weight:400;">company in history</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;">, an £8.2 Billion pounds bet intended to bring the company down. When you consider the top ten (and only the top ten) </span><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_oil_and_gas_companies_by_revenue"><span style="font-weight:400;">Oil and Natural Gas company </span></a><span style="font-weight:400;">accounted for 2.5 Trillion Pounds in revenue last year (2017), you can begin to understand why the disinformation is so prolific &amp; widespread.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">Recently a shameless anti-tesla </span><i><span style="font-weight:400;">Tweeter</span></i><span style="font-weight:400;"> was unveiled as a </span><a href="http://observer.com/2018/07/elon-musk-doxxes-tesla-critic/"><span style="font-weight:400;">major hedge fund manager</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;"> who was attempted to manipulate the market, attempting to pull the $TSLA stock down. This is far from an isolated example, the </span><a href="https://apnews.com/07c8fd1825ed42e08393f7d151a0664d"><span style="font-weight:400;">associative press</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;"> have reported the Trump administration attempting to curtail the </span><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Environmental_Protection_Agency"><span style="font-weight:400;">EPA’s </span></a><span style="font-weight:400;">strong stance on petrol &amp; diesel vehicle emissions by suggesting </span><i><span style="font-weight:400;">“people would drive more and be exposed to increased risk if their cars get better gas mileage”</span></i><span style="font-weight:400;">. Regardless whether you are pro or anti efficiency, </span><b>absolute nonsense</b><span style="font-weight:400;">. </span></p>
<p><a href="https://www.thesun.co.uk/news/5916630/theresa-may-nuclear-energy-electric-cars/"><i><span style="font-weight:400;">“Theresa May urged to build 10 more nuclear power plants to get millions of electric cars on the road”</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight:400;">, </span></i><span style="font-weight:400;">an article by the sun, brings us neatly back to the beginning. Not known for their lengthy attention span, </span><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_UK"><span style="font-weight:400;">News UK</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;"> have taken advantage of their readers by placing key information below the apparent end of the article &#8211; the union who </span><i><span style="font-weight:400;">‘urged Theresa May’</span></i><span style="font-weight:400;"> simply suggested more investment in the National Grid, which in the very next paragraph was disputed by the National Grid whom said, </span><i><span style="font-weight:400;">“The National Grid says it could cope with the ban on petrol and diesel cars as early as 2030.”</span></i></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">The fallacy our current infrastructure cannot support the transition to EV is rather two dimensional, it fails to take into account the very technology renewables and and EVs employ to make them viable. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight:400;">The National Grid (NG) have a rather exciting job, they employ </span><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruachan_Power_Station"><span style="font-weight:400;">huge dams in the mountains</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;">, enormous </span><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_Offshore_Wind_Farm"><span style="font-weight:400;">wind farms</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;">, </span><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaking_power_plant"><span style="font-weight:400;">peaking plants</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;"> (sigh). They quite literally and figuratively have too much power. That wind turbine you see doing roughly sod all, they are turned off &#8211; we’re generating too much power! Our problem is storing the enormous amount of power we receive in the UK.  The government are keen to move us towards a </span><a href="https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/publications-and-updates/ofgem-proposes-system-reforms-support-electric-vehicle-revolution"><span style="font-weight:400;">smart grid</span></a><span style="font-weight:400;"> which will enable EVs to charge when demand is lowest, or stop entirely if the grid is experiencing high demand. Simply put, EVs (and no doubt later home and industrial energy storage) can charge when Wind/Solar/Tide are supplying more than we consume, and when they don’t, stop charging &#8211; no blackouts, not even a flicker. Oh I should also mention most systems are designed to shut off from the grid shut off if they detect an imbalance,</span><b> it was never a problem to begin with.</b></p>
