<p>Hackernews has been fired up this evening by an<a href="https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=5465023"> interesting post</a> which was quickly rebuffed by an equally <a href="http://www.darianshimy.com/blog/2013/03/30/standups-are-not-poisonous/">passionate</a> VP, so why pray tell am I posting? I feel they&#8217;ve both somewhat missed the point.</p>
<p>Stand-ups are great when everyone takes a good dose of reality. Hackernews tends to attract entrepreneurs &amp; evangelical developers and that&#8217;s great! Listening to the experiences of weathered developers and starry-eyed entrepreneurs can introduce a brand new perspective that you may not have considered but it often forgets how the real world operates.</p>
<p>Strategy, Architecture, Engineering and are my core passions <del><em>(I like to build stuff).</em></del> There&#8217;s nothing like working with a team to build a product using ingenuity and foresight yet it has only ever half the picture &#8211; <em>if that</em>. Software engineering tends to take a rather 2 dimensional view of humans. Your engagement and interest just has to be binary &#8211; boredom or out right belligerence. Ask yourself, has that <em>ever</em> been true? another absolute maybe?</p>
<p><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuro-linguistic_programming">NLP</a> and our old friend Psychology provides us an entirely new arsenal of techniques to build engagement and stoke the fires of passion. NLP is considered to be snake-oil, another crack pot idea to flog some consulting hours right? The funny thing is much of these ideas are nothing more than forgotten wisdom, and it never hurts to have a subtle reminder <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_Patterns">right?</a> Let&#8217;s have a brief dust off:</p>
<ul>
<li>Humans are driven by survival. Anything which doesn&#8217;t trigger our fight or flight response requires will power</li>
<li>As we know, &#8216;will power&#8217; and slightly more loosely, engagement is actually a depletable resource. You can and will use it up.</li>
<li>The human brain processes 34GB of information per day. It is key to your survival that you only process what effects you</li>
</ul>
<p>This is all rather grandiose I hear you cry, well yes and that is no reason to ignore it. Let&#8217;s get back to the real point &#8211; how can you engage a team?</p>
<p><em><strong>A developer cannot stay on a card for more than two days</strong></em></p>
<p>Simple really<em> (strokes black cat).</em> The goal of a team is to <em>collaborate</em>. To successfully collaborate the entire team must be aware of what cards are in play. Now this is where the fun kicks in, if I don&#8217;t know which card I am going to play everything has just become a little random. Any ideas who you&#8217;ll be pairing with next? Don&#8217;t forget you&#8217;ll be driving!</p>
<p>Scared yet?</p>
