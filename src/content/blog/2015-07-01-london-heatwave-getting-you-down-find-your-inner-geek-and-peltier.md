<p>London is experiencing a rather unusual heatwave. Today the mercuary has tipped <a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-33324881">36.7 Celsius. </a> I hadn&#8217;t intended to blog about this today (working on an interesting, if long renewable piece) however!</p>

<p>Coincidentally enough I took apart a nice <a href="http://www.amazon.co.uk/Pro-Breeze%C2%AE-Portable-Dehumidifier-Moisture/dp/B00NFRTVY6/ref=sr_1_4?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1435771425&amp;sr=8-4&amp;keywords=dehumidifier">dehumidifier</a> to get hold of a rather useful component &#8211; the Peltier exchanger. What&#8217;s great about Pelter is they employ the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermoelectric_effect">thermoelectric effect</a> to cool, significantly, one side of the plate and heat the other.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="/blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png"><img loading="lazy" data-attachment-id="984" data-permalink="/blog/london-heatwave-getting-you-down-find-your-inner-geek-and-peltier/" data-orig-file="/blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png" data-orig-size="375,275" data-comments-opened="1" data-image-meta="{&quot;aperture&quot;:&quot;0&quot;,&quot;credit&quot;:&quot;&quot;,&quot;camera&quot;:&quot;&quot;,&quot;caption&quot;:&quot;&quot;,&quot;created_timestamp&quot;:&quot;0&quot;,&quot;copyright&quot;:&quot;&quot;,&quot;focal_length&quot;:&quot;0&quot;,&quot;iso&quot;:&quot;0&quot;,&quot;shutter_speed&quot;:&quot;0&quot;,&quot;title&quot;:&quot;&quot;,&quot;orientation&quot;:&quot;0&quot;}" data-image-title="peltier" data-image-description="" data-image-caption="" data-large-file="/blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-984" src="/blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png" alt="peltier" width="300" height="220" srcset="/blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png 300w, /blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png 150w, /blog-media/2015/07/peltier.png 375w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px" /></a>My plan is to build a cheap, but effective air-conditioning unit which will draw heat from my shed and exhaust it outside (I may scale this up a touch, so please forgive this experimental stage).</p>
<p>Like similar air conditioner/cooler you need a mechanism to vent the heat. What makes this particular device special is both it&#8217;s <a href="http://www.ebay.co.uk/sch/i.html?_from=R40&amp;_trksid=p2050601.m570.l1313.TR11.TRC2.A0.H0.Xpeltier.TRS0&amp;_nkw=peltier&amp;_sacat=0">accessibility and price</a>. Not only that is works on my favourite voltage 12VDC. Unfortunately it is a touch thirsty drawing quite a few amps when you get it going, but that&#8217;s a small price to pay to direct a nice chilling breeze at you&#8217;re otherwise tied brow.</p>
<p>In the above example I&#8217;ve hooked up a motorcycle battery to the Peltier module which was helpfully surrounded by two heat-sink-fan modules to extract heat and circulate the cool air.  You&#8217;ll have to forgive the aquarium thermostat which isn&#8217;t doing a great job of reporting the temperature, but still that&#8217;s 15C less than ambient.</p>
<p>While not as effective as a thousand pound air-conditioner, I&#8217;m quite satisfied with the results.</p>
