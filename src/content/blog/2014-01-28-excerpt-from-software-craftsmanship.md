<p>Here&#8217;s a great quote from &#8220;<em>Software Craftsmanship</em>&#8220;. Sandro picked up on this common cliché constantly included in countless books when discussing Agile.</p>
<blockquote><p>Agile coaches and consultancy companies like to sell to uninformed clients. The Toyota Dream. But firstly, building cars is totally different from building software. <strong>We do not use a car if it is not totally finished.</strong> We do not go back to the manufacturer after buying a car, and ask to add an extra door to it, or move the engine from the front to the back.</p></blockquote>
