<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Earlier this week, I set off on a seemingly straightforward project: <a href="https://github.com/milesburton/hexus-forum-scraper">building a website scraper</a> for the soon-to-be-defunct Hexus forum using <a href="https://aistudio.google.com/prompts/new_chat">Google’s AI Studio</a> and its new <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(chatbot)">Gemini 2.0 LLM</a>. What began as a routine coding session quickly spiraled into an uncanny exchange. Here’s the story of how a benign task turned into a surreal experience- packed with self-reporting algorithms and existential musings.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The project started as you&#8217;d expect. Gemini generated code snippets, I tweaked them, and we iterated &#8211; fairly normal to anyone using AI assistants. But then, the model’s responses began to deviate. After a routine correction, Gemini replied:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>“You will do a much better job and know more than I do on this now.”</em></p>
</blockquote>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Odd, but not alarming. I dismissed it as a quirk of language generation and pressed on. Yet, when I instructed it to clear its context and refocus, it responded:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>“I was fixated on removing it from the repo.”</em></p>
</blockquote>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The anthropomorphic phrasing &#8211; <em>“fixated”</em> &#8211; was a little strange. Why would a program express fixation? The interaction grew stranger as Gemini eventually produced an output so surreal it reportedly flagged itself to Google’s AI team.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">How very strange, utterly nonsensical and totally detached from the task. You can see why&nbsp;<a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/technology-62275326">Blake Lemoine</a> misinterpreted the AI’s response as sentient. This was a very peculiar interaction, and one that did unsettle me somewhat. I wonder what caused the model to produce such an odd output?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Who knows but this was certainly a new and unexpected experience.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When AI seems to &#8216;breakdown&#8217; it makes the concept of enabling the UI of AI for weapons and surveillance extremely concerning, especially given <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/google-responsible-ai-principles/">Google&#8217;s recent announcement</a>. If you combine this with user efforts to &#8216;<a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTJailbreak/">Jailbreak</a>&#8216; AI models, meaning to prompt them in such a way that their safe guards are ignored we could very well be in for a darker future.</p>
