Going from an M6 to M0 in four months is very rare, even more so for someone with little to no background in the hobby. That said, <strong>it is possible</strong>.</p>
<p>Let&#8217;s rewind back to my M6 days. This is a presentation I gave to the <a href="https://download.milesburton.com/Misc/My%20Path%20to%20M6LSZ.pptx">Thurrock Acorns</a> club.</p>
<p>Being an M6 wasn&#8217;t easy for me at all. For better or worse I don&#8217;t do things by halves; Sometimes this pays-off other times it all goes up in flames</p>
<p>My initial M6 days didn&#8217;t go overly smoothly. Mr Fox decided to destroy some rather expensive feeds. I even managed to short a feed in my haste costing me a not insignificant amount of money.</p>

<p>So you&#8217;re probably asking yourself why did I take the 2E0 and M0?</p>
<p>Ah this is an interesting question. I actually don&#8217;t spend a lot of time on HF. I&#8217;ve always been a tinkerer, enjoying experimenting with little raspberry pis, Beofengs and radiometrix ICs. Even before I started this little experiment I was writing packet radio software using Manchester encoding<em> (it was useless. Good learning though!)</em>.</p>
<p>I suppose the reason I continued the course was my comfort zone &#8211; I needed a challenge. I always wanted to work with electronics and design. I did make some headway into that area sometime ago when the Arduinos bootstrapped <em>(MnetHardware)</em>, however that still felt a bit superficial. The long story short is Amateur Radio is hard &#8211; I wanted to dust off the cobwebs.</p>
<p>I would love to say there is a secret approach to these licences &#8211; sadly there isn&#8217;t. It has taken a ridiculous amount of reading and watching videos.</p>
<p>I&#8217;ll say this. Even the complex chapters are simple given enough time. The creators who submitted their videos to <a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPBrzUDbMQ1VAbOg4yQljxi8co3d7gpRg">YouTube </a>are a god send. I&#8217;ve curated a small list which will help your progression to M0.</p>
<p>I wish you the best of luck.</p>
