<p>The idea of &#8216;Kata&#8217; or &#8216;Dojo&#8217; is really important. Even if you have zero interest finding a new job it always pays dividends to think, if I hired someone what would I expect them to know?</p>
<p>If you&#8217;ve haven&#8217;t spotted I&#8217;m a big fan of Groovy. You&#8217;re right, I&#8217;d have jumped on the Scala bandwagon (Akkta is full of win) though Groovy got there first.</p>
<p>So Groovy &amp; Grails (we&#8217;ll start with Groovy). What are the more unusual questions:</p>
<ul>
<li>How meta-classing implemented in Groovy?</li>
<li>What is the expandoMetaclass?</li>
<li>What is polymorphic dispatch? How is it useful?</li>
<li>How does groovy support AOP?</li>
<li>What is a closure? Name a couple of basic examples of where a closure could make your life easier?</li>
<li>What does the &#8216;it&#8217; reference refer to?</li>
<li>What are your opinions of Groovy&#8217;s performance? Is it slow?</li>
<li>Scala has the excellent Akkta framework, how does groovy compete?</li>
<li>What are your opinions of metaclassing? Why could it be bad?</li>
<li>What is the meta-object protocol? How may this affect TDD? Is there anything you need o watch out for?</li>
<li>What is your favorite testing tool for Groovy? How do you handle mocking?</li>
<li>How could you mock a static method?</li>
<li>How do you apply a metaclass to an entire hierarchy of polymorphic objects?</li>
<li>What is an AST? Which can you name? How else can an AST be useful?</li>
<li>Explain the concept behind an AST</li>
</ul>
