I'm a software engineer and maker based in the UK.

By day, I work on software systems. In my spare time, I tinker with embedded systems, contribute to open source, and occasionally write about what I've learned.

## Open Source

I'm the creator and maintainer of the [Arduino Temperature Control Library](https://github.com/milesburton/Arduino-Temperature-Control-Library), which has been helping makers read temperature data from Maxim/Dallas sensors since 2012.
