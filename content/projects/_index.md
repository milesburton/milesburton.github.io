## Arduino Temperature Control Library

**[GitHub](https://github.com/milesburton/Arduino-Temperature-Control-Library)** · 1000+ stars · Active since 2012

A robust Arduino library for Maxim/Dallas temperature sensors (DS18B20, DS18S20, DS1822, MAX31850, and more). Supports multiple sensors on a single bus, asynchronous readings, and configurable resolution.

Used by thousands of makers worldwide for weather stations, home automation, brewing, and industrial monitoring.
